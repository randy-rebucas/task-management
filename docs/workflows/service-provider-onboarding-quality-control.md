# Service Provider Onboarding & Quality Control Workflow


## Overview
This document describes the workflow for the Service Provider Onboarding & Quality Control role, including key stages, touchpoints, and best practices for ensuring quality and compliance.

## Workflow Diagram
```mermaid
flowchart LR
	A[Provider Sourcing] --> B[Onboarding]
	B --> C[Quality Assessment]
	C --> D[Ongoing Monitoring]
```

## Typical Workflow Stages
1. **Provider Sourcing:** Identify and evaluate potential service providers.
2. **Onboarding:** Complete documentation, training, and system setup.
3. **Quality Assessment:** Perform initial and ongoing quality checks.
4. **Ongoing Monitoring:** Continuously monitor performance and compliance.

## Key Responsibilities
- Source and onboard providers
- Assess and monitor quality
- Ensure compliance with standards
- Provide ongoing support
- Maintain provider records

## Example Transitions
- Provider Sourcing → Onboarding
- Onboarding → Quality Assessment
- Quality Assessment → Ongoing Monitoring

## Touchpoints
- Onboarding portal
- Quality assessment tools
- Compliance dashboards

## Best Practices
- Standardize onboarding steps
- Automate quality checks
- Provide ongoing training
- Maintain clear documentation
