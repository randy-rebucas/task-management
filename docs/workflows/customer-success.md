# Customer Success Workflow


## Overview
This document describes the workflow for the Customer Success role, including key stages, touchpoints, and best practices for maximizing customer satisfaction.

## Workflow Diagram
```mermaid
flowchart LR
	A[Onboarding] --> B[Engagement & Support]
	B --> C[Feedback Collection]
	C --> D[Renewal & Upsell]
```

## Typical Workflow Stages
1. **Onboarding:** Welcome users, set expectations, and provide initial training.
2. **Engagement & Support:** Maintain regular contact, resolve issues, and offer guidance.
3. **Feedback Collection:** Gather user feedback through surveys and direct communication.
4. **Renewal & Upsell:** Identify opportunities for renewal and additional services.

## Key Responsibilities
- Guide customers through onboarding
- Address customer issues
- Collect and act on feedback
- Monitor customer health metrics
- Proactively identify at-risk customers

## Example Transitions
- Onboarding → Engagement & Support
- Engagement & Support → Feedback Collection
- Feedback Collection → Renewal & Upsell

## Touchpoints
- Onboarding sessions
- Support tickets/chat
- Customer satisfaction surveys

## Best Practices
- Personalize onboarding
- Track user health metrics
- Close the loop on feedback
- Proactively engage with customers
