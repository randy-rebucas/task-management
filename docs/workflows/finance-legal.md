# Finance & Legal Workflow


## Overview
This document describes the workflow for the Finance & Legal role, including key stages, touchpoints, and best practices for compliance and efficiency.

## Workflow Diagram
```mermaid
flowchart LR
	A[Budget Planning] --> B[Transaction Processing]
	B --> C[Compliance Review]
	C --> D[Reporting]
```

## Typical Workflow Stages
1. **Budget Planning:** Set financial goals, allocate budgets, and forecast needs.
2. **Transaction Processing:** Process payments, reimbursements, and contracts.
3. **Compliance Review:** Ensure all transactions meet legal and regulatory standards.
4. **Reporting:** Prepare and distribute financial and legal reports.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage budgets and expenses
- Ensure legal compliance
- Prepare financial reports
- Review contracts and agreements
- Maintain audit trails

## Example Transitions
- Budget Planning → Transaction Processing
- Transaction Processing → Compliance Review
- Compliance Review → Reporting

## Touchpoints
- Submission forms
- Approval workflows
- Automated status updates

## Best Practices
- Ensure transparency in processing
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Maintain compliance documentation
- Regularly audit financial processes
