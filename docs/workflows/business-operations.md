# Business Operations Workflow


## Overview
This document describes the workflow for the Business Operations role, including key stages, touchpoints, and best practices for operational excellence.

## Workflow Diagram
```mermaid
flowchart LR
	A[Intake & Planning] --> B[Resource Allocation]
	B --> C[Execution & Monitoring]
	C --> D[Reporting & Optimization]
```

## Typical Workflow Stages
1. **Intake & Planning:** Capture business needs, set objectives, and define scope.
2. **Resource Allocation:** Assign teams, budget, and tools to tasks.
3. **Execution & Monitoring:** Implement plans, track progress, and resolve issues.
4. **Reporting & Optimization:** Analyze outcomes, report to stakeholders, and optimize processes.

## Key Responsibilities
- Oversee daily business processes
- Coordinate between departments
- Ensure compliance and efficiency
- Monitor KPIs and operational metrics
- Drive process improvements

## Example Transitions
- Intake & Planning → Resource Allocation
- Resource Allocation → Execution & Monitoring
- Execution & Monitoring → Reporting & Optimization

## Touchpoints
- Request submission portal
- Status dashboards
- Automated notifications

## Best Practices
- Maintain clear communication channels
- Use data-driven decision making
- Regularly review and optimize processes
- Foster cross-departmental collaboration
