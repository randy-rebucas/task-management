# Sales & Partnerships Workflow


## Overview
This document describes the workflow for the Sales & Partnerships role, including key stages, touchpoints, and best practices for building strong relationships.

## Workflow Diagram
```mermaid
flowchart LR
	A[Lead Generation] --> B[Qualification]
	B --> C[Proposal & Negotiation]
	C --> D[Closing & Onboarding]
```

## Typical Workflow Stages
1. **Lead Generation:** Identify and attract potential clients or partners.
2. **Qualification:** Assess leads for fit and readiness.
3. **Proposal & Negotiation:** Present solutions and negotiate terms.
4. **Closing & Onboarding:** Finalize agreements and onboard new clients/partners.

## Key Responsibilities
- Generate and qualify leads
- Negotiate deals
- Onboard new partners
- Maintain CRM records
- Build long-term relationships

## Example Transitions
- Lead Generation → Qualification
- Qualification → Proposal & Negotiation
- Proposal & Negotiation → Closing & Onboarding

## Touchpoints
- CRM system
- Email/phone communication
- Onboarding materials

## Best Practices
- Qualify leads early
- Document all interactions
- Provide clear onboarding resources
- Follow up consistently
