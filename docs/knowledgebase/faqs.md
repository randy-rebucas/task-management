# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

This document answers the most common questions from team members across all departments.

---

## General

**Q: How do I log in to the system?**
Go to the login page, enter your registered email and password. If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot Password** and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Click **Forgot Password** on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. Passwords expire after 90 days.

**Q: How do I update my profile?**
Navigate to **Settings** from the top menu, then edit your name, avatar, phone number, and job title.

---

## Tasks

**Q: How do I create a task?**
Go to **Tasks** in the sidebar, click **New Task**, fill in the required fields (title, assignee, due date, priority), and click **Save**.

**Q: How do I assign a task to someone else?**
When creating or editing a task, select the appropriate person from the **Assignee** field.

**Q: How do I move a task to a different status?**
Open the task and use the **Status** dropdown or workflow transition buttons to move it to the next stage. Some transitions may require remarks or approval.

**Q: Can I attach files to a task?**
Yes. Open the task and use the **Attachments** section to upload files.

**Q: How do I log time on a task?**
Open the task and click **Log Time**. Enter the duration and notes, then save.

---

## Workflows

**Q: What is a workflow?**
A workflow defines the stages a task must pass through, ensuring consistent processes. See the [Glossary](./glossary.md) or individual workflow documentation for details.

**Q: Why can't I transition a task to a certain status?**
Some transitions are restricted to specific roles or require approval. Contact your team lead if you believe you should have access.

**Q: What happens when a task requires approval?**
The system will notify the designated approvers. The task cannot proceed until it is approved or rejected.

---

## Notifications

**Q: How do I manage my notifications?**
Go to **Settings > Notifications** to customize which events trigger alerts.

**Q: I'm getting too many notifications. What can I do?**
Adjust your notification preferences in **Settings > Notifications** to reduce or filter the types of alerts you receive.

---

## Permissions & Access

**Q: I can't access a feature I need. What do I do?**
Submit a request to your department head, who will coordinate with the Tech & Product team or administrator. See [Permissions & Access](./permissions-and-access.md).

**Q: How are roles assigned?**
Roles are assigned by administrators. Contact IT or your manager if your role needs to be updated.

**Q: Can I have more than one role?**
Yes. Users can be assigned multiple roles and will inherit the combined permissions of all their roles.

---

## CRM & Leads

**Q: How do I add a new lead?**
Navigate to **CRM > Leads**, click **New Lead**, fill in the details, and save.

**Q: How do I convert a lead to a client?**
Open the lead record and use the **Convert to Client** option once the lead is qualified.

---

## Reports & Analytics

**Q: How do I generate a report?**
Go to **Reports**, select the report type and date range, apply filters if needed, and click **Generate**.

**Q: Can I export reports?**
Yes. Most reports have an **Export** option (PDF or CSV).

---

## Technical Issues

**Q: The system is slow or unresponsive. What should I do?**
Try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or try a different browser. If still unresolved, contact the Tech & Product team.

**Q: I found a bug. How do I report it?**
Submit a bug report through the support channel or create a task assigned to the Tech & Product team with detailed steps to reproduce the issue.

**Q: Who do I contact for urgent system issues?**
Escalate to the Tech & Product team immediately. For critical issues, follow the [Escalation Policy](./escalation-policy.md).

---

## Performance & KPIs

**Q: How do I view my performance targets?**
Go to **Performance** in the sidebar to see your personal KPI targets, progress, and history.

**Q: Who sets my performance targets?**
Your department manager or team lead sets your performance targets, usually at the start of each month or quarter.

**Q: Can I track team performance?**
Managers can view team-wide performance data from the **Performance** module or **Analytics** dashboard.

---

## Field Monitoring

**Q: How do I start a field session?**
Navigate to **Field Monitoring**, click **Start Session**, and fill in your location and purpose.

**Q: How do I submit proof of work?**
Go to **Proof of Work**, click **+ Submit Proof**, upload photos, fill in task details, and submit.

**Q: Who can see my field activity?**
Your manager and authorized team members with field monitoring access can view your sessions.

---

## Calendar

**Q: How does the calendar work?**
The calendar shows all your assigned tasks and events in a visual date format. You can switch between day, week, and month views.

**Q: Can I add events to the calendar?**
Tasks with due dates automatically appear in the calendar based on their assigned dates.

---

## Activity Log

**Q: What is the activity log?**
The activity log records every action performed in the system — task updates, status changes, logins, and more — for audit and accountability.

**Q: Who can view the activity log?**
Administrators and Business Operations team members with the `activity:view` permission.

---

## Departments & Staff

**Q: How do I view my department members?**
Go to **Staff** or **Departments** in the sidebar to see team members and their roles.

**Q: How do I add a new staff member?**
Administrators or authorized users can go to **Staff > Add Staff**, fill in the required details, and assign a role and department.
