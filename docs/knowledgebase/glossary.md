# Glossary

This glossary defines key terms and concepts used across the organization and the task management platform.

---

## A

**Activity Log**
A record of all actions performed in the system, including task updates, status changes, and user actions. Used for auditing and accountability.

**Analytics**
The collection and analysis of data to measure performance, identify trends, and support decision-making.

**API (Application Programming Interface)**
A set of rules that allows different software systems to communicate with each other.

**Approval**
A workflow step where a designated person must review and authorize a task or transition before it can proceed.

---

## B

**Backlog**
A list of tasks or features that are planned but not yet started.

**Budget**
The financial plan allocated to a department, project, or campaign.

---

## C

**Campaign**
A coordinated set of marketing or sales activities aimed at achieving a specific goal.

**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**
A system for managing interactions with current and potential clients, leads, and partners.

**Curriculum**
A structured set of learning materials and activities designed for training purposes.

---

## D

**Dashboard**
The main interface of the platform showing a summary of tasks, KPIs, activities, and notifications.

**Deal**
A sales opportunity associated with a client or lead, tracked through defined stages.

**Department**
An organizational unit responsible for a specific function (e.g., Finance, Marketing).

---

## E

**Escalation**
The process of raising an unresolved issue to a higher level of authority or expertise.

---

## F

**Field Monitoring**
Tracking and logging the activities of staff who work outside the office.

**Feedback**
Information provided by users, clients, or staff about their experience, used for improvement.

---

## K

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**
A measurable value that indicates how effectively an individual, team, or organization is achieving objectives.

---

## L

**Lead**
A potential client or partner who has shown interest but has not yet converted.

**LMS (Learning Management System)**
A software platform used to create, deliver, and track training programs.

---

## N

**Notification**
An alert sent to a user about an event such as a task assignment, deadline, or status change.

---

## O

**Onboarding**
The process of integrating a new user, employee, client, or service provider into the organization or system.

---

## P

**Permission**
A specific action that a user is authorized to perform, defined as `resource:action` (e.g., `tasks:create`).

**Proof of Work**
Documentation or evidence submitted by field staff to confirm completed tasks or visits.

---

## R

**RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)**
A security model where access to features and data is granted based on the user's assigned role.

**Role**
A defined collection of permissions assigned to a user that determines their level of access within the system.

---

## S

**Service Provider**
An external individual or organization contracted to deliver services on behalf of the organization.

**SLA (Service Level Agreement)**
A commitment between a service provider and a client defining expected service levels, response times, and quality standards.

**Slug**
A URL-friendly text identifier derived from a name (e.g., `business-operations`). Used for routing and identification.

**Status**
The current stage of a task or workflow item (e.g., Pending, In Progress, Completed, On Hold).

**Subtask**
A smaller unit of work nested under a parent task to break down complex work into manageable steps.

---

## T

**Task**
A unit of work assigned to one or more team members with a defined title, deadline, priority, and status.

**Touchpoint**
Any point of contact or interaction between a user and the organization or system.

**Transition**
A workflow action that moves a task from one status to another, sometimes requiring remarks or approval.

---

## U

**UAT (User Acceptance Testing)**
A phase in product development where real users validate that a solution meets requirements before release.

**Upsell**
Offering a customer an upgraded or additional service beyond what they currently have.

---

## V

**Visit Log**
A record of physical or virtual visits made by field staff, documenting purpose, people met, and outcomes.

---

## W

**Workflow**
A defined sequence of statuses and transitions that a task or process moves through from initiation to completion.

**Workflow Transition**
A rule that defines which status changes are allowed, who can make them, and what conditions must be met.
