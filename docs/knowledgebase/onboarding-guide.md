# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the organization! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get started, from your first day through your first month.

---

## Before Day 1

- Receive your login credentials from HR/IT
- Review this knowledgebase
- Confirm your department and reporting manager
- Set up your workstation and communication tools

---

## Day 1: Getting Started

### Step 1 – First Login
1. Go to the platform login page
2. Enter your email and temporary password
3. Change your password immediately
4. Update your profile (name, avatar, contact info)

### Step 2 – Explore the Dashboard
- Familiarize yourself with the navigation menu
- Review the [System Guide](./system-guide.md) for a full module overview
- Check for any tasks already assigned to you

### Step 3 – Meet Your Team
- Attend the team introduction session
- Review the [Organization Overview](./organization-overview.md) and [Roles & Responsibilities](./roles-and-responsibilities.md)
- Connect with your direct manager and teammates

---

## Week 1: Learning the Basics

| Day       | Focus                                                  |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Day 1     | System orientation and profile setup                   |
| Day 2     | Review your department's workflow documentation        |
| Day 3     | Shadow a senior team member                            |
| Day 4     | Attend team meetings and stand-ups                     |
| Day 5     | Complete initial tasks and review feedback             |

  - [Academy / Training Division](../workflows/academy-training-division.md)
- Begin contributing independently to team tasks
- Provide feedback on your onboarding experience

---

## 30 / 60 / 90 Day Plan

### First 30 Days – Learn
- Complete all onboarding steps
- Learn the platform and department workflow
- Shadow senior teammates
- Attend all team meetings
- Identify key contacts and stakeholders

### Days 31–60 – Contribute
- Start handling assigned tasks independently
- Participate in team discussions and initiatives
- Identify areas for improvement in your workflow
- Begin building relationships with cross-functional teams

### Days 61–90 – Lead
- Take ownership of key responsibilities
- Suggest process improvements based on observations
- Set personal KPI targets with your manager
- Complete any required training from the Academy

## Buddy System

New team members are paired with a **buddy** — an experienced colleague who helps with:
- System navigation
- Culture and team norms
- Answering day-to-day questions
- Introductions to key stakeholders

Reach out to your manager to confirm your assigned buddy on Day 1.
