# Organization Overview

## Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** Deliver exceptional service and operational excellence to clients, partners, and staff through efficient systems and collaboration.
- **Vision:** Be the leading platform for streamlined business operations, customer success, and growth.

---

## Core Values
- **Integrity** – Act with honesty and transparency in all interactions.
- **Collaboration** – Work together to achieve shared goals.
- **Excellence** – Continuously improve and deliver quality results.
- **Innovation** – Embrace change and drive progress.
- **Accountability** – Own your work and its outcomes.

## Department Summaries

### Business Operations
Oversees and coordinates day-to-day business functions, ensures process efficiency, and drives cross-departmental alignment.

### Customer Success
Manages the full customer lifecycle — from onboarding to renewal — ensuring satisfaction and long-term relationships.

### Finance & Legal
Handles budgeting, financial reporting, transaction processing, compliance, and legal documentation.

### Marketing & Growth
Plans and executes campaigns, manages brand presence, drives lead generation, and analyzes growth metrics.

### Sales & Partnerships
Generates leads, qualifies prospects, negotiates deals, and onboards new clients and partners.

### Service Provider Onboarding & Quality Control
Sources, onboards, and monitors service providers, ensuring quality and compliance with organizational standards.

### Tech & Product
Designs, develops, and maintains all internal and external technology products, platforms, and infrastructure.

### Academy / Training Division
Identifies training needs, designs curricula, delivers learning programs, and evaluates training effectiveness.

## Interdepartmental Collaboration

Effective collaboration between departments ensures the organization operates as a unified whole. Below are key collaboration touchpoints:

| Departments | Collaboration Area |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Sales & Customer Success | Smooth handoff from closing to onboarding |
| Marketing & Sales | Lead generation alignment and campaign support |
| Tech & Product + All Departments | System support, feature requests, and integrations |
| Finance & All Departments | Budget approvals, expense management |
| Business Operations + All Departments | Process coordination and reporting |
| Academy + All Departments | Training needs and skills development |
| SP Onboarding & QC + Customer Success | Provider performance and issue resolution |

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Each department tracks specific KPIs to measure success:

| Department | KPIs |
|------------|------|
| Business Operations | Process turnaround time, SLA compliance, operational cost |
| Customer Success | Customer satisfaction score (CSAT), churn rate, NPS |
| Finance & Legal | Budget variance, compliance rate, invoice processing time |
| Marketing & Growth | Leads generated, campaign ROI, conversion rate |
| Sales & Partnerships | Revenue closed, number of deals, partner growth |
| SP Onboarding & QC | Onboarding time, quality score, provider compliance rate |
| Tech & Product | System uptime, bug resolution time, feature delivery rate |
| Academy / Training | Training completion rate, knowledge retention, learner satisfaction |

## Operational Principles

1. **Transparency** – Share information openly to enable informed decisions.
2. **Efficiency** – Eliminate waste and optimize processes continuously.
3. **Customer-Centricity** – Place the customer's needs at the heart of every decision.
4. **Data-Driven** – Ground decisions in data and measurable outcomes.
5. **Continuous Improvement** – Treat every process as an opportunity to improve.
6. **Cross-Functional Thinking** – Consider how actions impact all departments.
