# Roles & Responsibilities

This document outlines the key roles in the organization and their associated responsibilities, permissions, and collaboration expectations.

---

## 1. Business Operations

**Role Purpose:** Ensure smooth day-to-day operations across all functions.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Process Management    | Oversee and optimize business processes                      |
| Cross-dept Coordination | Align departments on shared objectives                    |
| Compliance            | Ensure adherence to policies and standards                   |
| KPI Monitoring        | Track and report operational performance metrics             |
| Resource Planning     | Allocate resources efficiently across teams                  |

---

## 2. Customer Success

**Role Purpose:** Maximize customer satisfaction and long-term value.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Onboarding            | Guide new customers through setup and initial use            |
| Engagement            | Maintain proactive communication with customers              |
| Issue Resolution      | Address and resolve customer concerns promptly               |
| Feedback Management   | Collect, categorize, and act on customer feedback            |
| Renewals & Upsells    | Identify and pursue opportunities for growth                 |

---

## 3. Finance & Legal

**Role Purpose:** Manage financial health and legal compliance of the organization.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Budgeting             | Plan and manage budgets across departments                   |
| Transaction Processing| Handle payments, invoices, and reimbursements                |
| Compliance Review     | Ensure all activities comply with laws and regulations       |
| Contract Management   | Draft, review, and manage legal agreements                   |
| Financial Reporting   | Prepare and distribute financial statements                  |

---

## 4. Marketing & Growth

**Role Purpose:** Drive brand visibility, lead generation, and revenue growth.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Campaign Planning     | Define strategy, goals, and target audience                  |
| Content Creation      | Produce marketing materials across all channels              |
| Campaign Execution    | Launch and manage campaigns                                  |
| Analytics             | Monitor and report on campaign performance                   |
| Market Research       | Identify trends, opportunities, and competitor insights      |

---

## 5. Sales & Partnerships

**Role Purpose:** Grow revenue through new clients and strategic partnerships.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Lead Generation       | Identify and attract potential clients/partners              |
| Qualification         | Assess lead readiness and fit                                |
| Proposal Writing      | Prepare tailored proposals                                   |
| Negotiation           | Negotiate deal terms to mutual benefit                       |
| Client Onboarding     | Transition new clients into the system smoothly              |

---

## 6. Service Provider Onboarding & Quality Control

**Role Purpose:** Ensure high-quality, compliant service providers.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Provider Sourcing     | Identify and vet potential service providers                 |
| Onboarding            | Complete documentation, training, and setup                  |
| Quality Assessment    | Conduct performance and quality evaluations                  |
| Compliance Monitoring | Ensure providers meet regulatory and organizational standards|
| Support               | Provide ongoing guidance to service providers                |

---

## 7. Tech & Product

**Role Purpose:** Build, maintain, and improve all technology products and infrastructure.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Requirements Gathering| Collect and document stakeholder needs                       |
| Design & Architecture | Plan system and product design                               |
| Development           | Build and iterate on features                                |
| Testing & QA          | Ensure quality through structured testing                    |
| Deployment & Support  | Release products and manage post-launch support              |

---

## 8. Academy / Training Division

**Role Purpose:** Build organizational capability through structured learning programs.

| Responsibility        | Description                                                  |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Needs Assessment      | Identify gaps in skills and knowledge                        |
| Curriculum Design     | Create structured and engaging training content              |
| Training Delivery     | Facilitate workshops, sessions, and e-learning               |
| Learner Tracking      | Monitor progress and completion                              |
| Evaluation            | Assess training effectiveness and iterate                    |

## Collaboration Expectations

Each role is expected to collaborate cross-functionally. Below are the key collaboration points per role:

| Role | Primary Collaborators | Key Collaboration Areas |
|------|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Business Operations | All departments | Process improvement, reporting, compliance |
| Customer Success | Sales, Tech & Product | Handoffs, product feedback, issue resolution |
| Finance & Legal | Business Operations, Sales | Budgets, contracts, reimbursements |
| Marketing & Growth | Sales, Tech & Product | Campaign support, lead handoff, analytics |
| Sales & Partnerships | Marketing, Customer Success | Qualified leads, client onboarding |
| SP Onboarding & QC | Business Operations | Provider performance, compliance |
| Tech & Product | All departments | System requests, support, deployments |
| Academy / Training | All departments | Training needs, curriculum delivery |
